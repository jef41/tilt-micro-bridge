# Case design
The files here are for 3D printing a simple case. 

I suggest purchasing a Pico with header pins presoldered. The case is designed for a snug fit, if you have soldered the pins yourself you may need to trim the pins on the soldered side.

The image shows how the parts are intended to fit together;

<img src="../misc/case_v2.png" alt="case assembly, showing main body of case, wedge and door" height="250px">

Slide the assembled Pico and display into the case. At the blank end of the case there is a wedge which will lift the display to meet the front of the case. Then insert the wedge at the open end of the case, this will lift the left side of the LCD to meet the case. Finally slide the door, which will hold everything in place.

The base contains two recesses into which I dropped a couple of magnets - just to give the case some stability, since I usually place this on a metal surface.

When printing you may wish to place the case on one end. In this orientation the only support required is around the window. Placing support blockers around the magnet recesses will significantly reduce the effort required in removing supports after printing.
